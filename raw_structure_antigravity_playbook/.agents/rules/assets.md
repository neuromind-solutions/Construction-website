---
trigger: model_decision
description: Apply when selecting, importing, editing, or documenting images, video, fonts, icons, or other media.
---
-   Record the source and licence/permission status of each nontrivial
    asset in `ASSET_REGISTER.csv`.
-   Never assume a publicly viewable asset is free to download, modify,
    or use commercially.
-   Do not extract protected or private template assets or bypass access
    controls.
-   Prefer owner-provided, original, public-domain, or appropriately
    licensed assets.
-   Keep asset dimensions and focal point responsive; do not distort
    images.
-   Do not use a stock asset if its content materially misrepresents a
    real project.
