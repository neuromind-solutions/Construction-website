conventions. - Current Next.js setup and project structure guidance. -

understood. 5. If docs conflict, explain the conflict and recommend the
safer supported path. 6. Do not treat search snippets as conclusive
evidence. 7. Update `SOURCES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
