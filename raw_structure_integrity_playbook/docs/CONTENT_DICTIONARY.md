### Section: (01) OUR STORY
```
Raw Structure was founded in 1987 by Hendrik Raaijmakers, a site foreman who was tired
of watching good buildings get ruined by bad sequencing. The first job was a harbour
warehouse in Rotterdam. It finished eleven days early.

Through the nineties we grew the way buildings grow — one floor at a time. Structural
steel came in-house in 1996, civil works followed in 2004, and in 2013 we opened our
first offices in New York and Oslo.

Today we are 640 engineers, foremen and trades working across three continents. We
still hire people who can read a drawing and swing a hammer, and we still publish
every safety statistic we record — good or bad.

We do not chase the biggest contracts. We chase the ones where precision matters:
live hospitals, heritage shells, bridges over working rivers. The places where a
shortcut is not an option.
```
